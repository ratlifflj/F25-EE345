The [discord server](https://discord.gg/RFyExHSZuS) for this course has been created. Feel free to join; the course staff will use it to make annoucements and engage with students questions. It is also a good place to discuss homework problems, lectures, and Python exercises. 

LaTeX bots have been added to render equations nicely! The MathBot also allows you to query WolframAlpha and have the response integrated into the chat.
